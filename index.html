System design, DevOps and cloud

Building the feature is half the job. The other half is designing it to scale, deploying it safely, and keeping it running.

System design

  • Scalable backend architectureClear service boundaries, clean APIs, and choices that hold up as traffic and teams grow.
  • Databases at scaleReplication, partitioning and sharding, and knowing when each one is worth the cost.
  • Consistency and availability trade-offsCAP and PACELC applied to real products, such as booking and payments.
  • Caching, CDNs and networkingCutting latency and absorbing traffic spikes and attacks at the edge.

DevOps and cloud

  • Cloud deploymentShipping backends to the cloud with repeatable, low-risk releases.
  • CI/CD and release processAutomated build, test and deploy steps so small changes reach production safely.
  • Monitoring and incident responseAlerts, incident SLAs and war rooms, so outages get fixed fast and the team learns from them.
  • Cost and capacityPlanning capacity and estimating cost before a feature is committed.
